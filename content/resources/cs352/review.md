> Please note this module review is based on teaching in the 2025/26 academic year. Remember that module contents, organisers, courseworks and exams are all subject to change.

## 1 - Why did I pick this module?

Had to, it's required for the Masters programme.

## 2 - Workload

This is one of those modules that doesn't require you to think very hard, the most difficult part for most people will be either dealing with group work or nailing down all the concepts you're expected to replicate in the exam. It does require some proactivity during the term, but the actual content itself isn't hard - it just requires good levels of memorisation and application, which will no doubt flare up when it comes to revision time.

## 3 - Assessments

There are three assessments: a group presentation (eek!), an individual report and an exam.

### Group Presentation

Essentially, you make a pitch to handle a project, which you will then go through the motions of making a presentation for, in layman's terms, explaining how you'd do the thing. This is all quite dependent on your group and your overall level of discipline/accountability, but by itself, this isn't difficult. You'll present in front of a "project board", and you'll get the results fairly soon afterwards.

### Individual Report

*Alongside* doing your presentation, you also do a four-stage report going over how you managed the project, showing that you know what you're talking about, as well as providing your own critical perspective of the team. This one is very dependent on your critical thinking and your ability to apply the concepts used. Plenty doable, but given the report is marked by your peers, you'll learn quickly what differentiates a good report from a great one. It *is* possible to get screwed over by someone who didn't mark properly.

### Exam

The exam was a little more difficult than previous years, with some questions presenting as edge-case style questions that weren't so easy to quickly churn out. That being said, it wasn't too hard to do decently well, there's a mix of written comprehension, critical analysis and applying techniques from lectures.

## 4 - Teaching Quality

Teaching is overall quite decent, with pacing being good, if not a bit dull at times, though this is more due to the content rather than the lecturer. The lecturer clearly does a lot to make the module engaging, however this can come across as akin to that "corporate friendliness" that may rub some people the wrong way. Seminars were mixed, with those taught only by the teaching assistants being lacklustre since these often were cases of "just get the right answer, we won't explain why it's right".

## 5 - Content and Difficulty

The content is rather boring, and there's a lot of it. The concepts themselves aren't hard to get your head around at all, but again, there's simply just a lot of it, and you're expected to make use of it all, which is where the difficulty comes in. Not the most difficult module you'll ever do by a long shot, but don't write it off as zero-effort, otherwise you will end up with the short end of the stick.

## 6 - Did I actually enjoy it?

Not really, but I think that's beside the point. I very much roll my eyes with this module, a lot of it is corporate slop which demands the use of certain frameworks with fancy names and some dubious descriptions of personalities and roles. However, I did learn some neat things from this module, such as the fact that it's perfectly valid for a project to fail, the point is the lessons learned from it. I also picked up some techniques such as a small amount of Kanban when I organise my tasks for a day.

## 7 - Who's this module for?

Anyone who wants to pad out their options with something that'll lend some useful tips and is teeming at the seams with the smell of cheap over-roasted coffee, overenthusiastic bosses and software engineers on the verge of a nervous breakdown. In other words, it's a harder business school module.

## 8 - The big tip

Practice the techniques. You'll slowly get better at them over time and this will help you not only in the exam, but the report as well.

## Final Verdict

5/10 difficulty, may present difficulty if you're too used to business school.

I didn't enjoy this module much, but I learned some good practical things from it. Could've been better, could've been worse, very middling. 5/10.
