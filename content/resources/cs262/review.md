To be honest, I had no idea what this module would be about, but to put it simply, it's just boolean logic. Let's see how far they can stretch this one out.

## Teaching

The teaching for this module was poor this year, mainly since we had a new organiser this year. That being said, they clearly showed up to lectures unprepared, mainly reading off slides, and making very frequent errors in any examples attempted: it was clear that they had not consolidated their own knowledge before each lecture, so everyone felt a bit clueless with the content at times (although I may be projecting in saying that).

## Seminars

The seminars were not useful, as in my case, the tutor only covered the boolean logic problems, which I found to be somewhat trivial. They made it clear that they would not cover the prolog content, which I did struggle with, which proved frustrating.

## Labs and Coursework
 
Labs were purely dedicated to the coursework, which is in Prolog. I'll make this simple: after doing this module, **I f\*\*\*ing hate prolog**. It sucks SO MUCH.

## Exam

The exam was of similar difficulty and structure to previous years.
