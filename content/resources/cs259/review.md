## My Thoughts

This year represents a small pivot, since we have had a new module organiser this year.

### Teaching

**You need to be at lectures for this module**, as the dreaded blackboards have returned. There are slides that were used in previous years, but I did not really refer to these. That being said, the teaching is fairly well-paced and the lecturer made sure to make frequent stops to make sure everyone was on the same page in terms of understanding. My only criticism is that some topics flowed into each other, or extended over multiple sessions, leading to inconsistent pacing in my handwritten notes (hence the creation of "she turing my machine till I accepting state").

### Seminars

The seminars for this module were not particularly engaging, either because I found the content of the sheets too easy, or because explanations of solutions were dragged out or not easy to understand. I frequently caught myself either phasing out or checking the UWCS discord way too many times.

### Coursework

I was able to work through the coursework at a fairly consistent pace, making it satisfying to work on. If you've got a strong understanding of Java, you should be able to get through this work just fine once you've grasped what you need to write in terms of theory.

### Class Test

The class test is fairly easy, and even open book, so there's not much to worry about. I very much hate the fact that wrong answers are punished with score reductions, though. Not cool.

### Exam

The exam this year was **much harder** than in previous years, and most people I've talked to seem to agree. This is likely because of the fact that this module has a new organiser this year (who has been known to write some brutal exams...).
