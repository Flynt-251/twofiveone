# This is twofiveone.

This is merely the corner of the internet of a tech nerd who's trying to do the right thing. But it's also a little more than that. It's a centre for free information, learning, and a moment of calm away from the ad-ridden, tracker-infested modern internet.

I, Flynt, run this site and on occasion, post stuff I think might be interesting or useful. I personally enjoy sharing stories where I've learned something, created a cool project, or just share something I already know and will benefit you, the reader. All without trying to use frivolous vocabulary and assume you know everything about some topic or another, like so many of us do.

## A force for change.

I'm currently one of the lead organisers for [Warwick Speedrunning](https://warwickspeed.run), the UK's largest student-led speedrunning event, where runners all across the UK come together and raise money for SpecialEffect, a cause we've been more than happy to work with for over ten years. In 2026, the event raised a jaw-dropping **£5,036.67** over just two days - more than doubling our previous record, all while live streaming some of the best and most entertaining speedruns I've ever seen. To be at the helm of such a fantastic event is an honour.

I've also been one of the Gaming Events Coordinators and now patron of [UWCS](https://uwcs.co.uk), where I started helping for this event, as well as organise LAN parties and gaming nights for students - a great way for all of us to unwind after a stressful week.

Additionally, I've done work as a coding tutor with Warwick Coding Society, and as a teaching assistant for after-school revision sessions of GCSE Computer Science with my secondary school, as I wanted to share the great depths of knowledge I'd gained from studying.

## A Bastion of Digital Autonomy.

Technology demands all of our attention nowadays, enough to where we don't even have the time to stop and think about the risks. People's data is collected without them understanding how, products are sold to us using snake oil claims, and governments set up technology programmes like "AI training" without addressing the issues of digital access. Today's tech companies want you to use their tools, not understand them.

Twofiveone fights against this. It should be up to you how much data you give online, to understand products enough to know whether the marketing is misleading you. Every tool has its use. But in the current landscape, it's like you're being told to use a table saw, when you've never even held a saw before. This website contains vast resources to help you understand how computers actually work, and how various technologies work, and it's continually being updated.

In a time where vast amounts of data is harvested without people's consent, digital autonomy matters more than ever.

## A means of exploration.

Twofiveone started as a portfolio website. It took two years for me to understand that in order to stand out, that wouldn't do. Instead, one has to take a stance, and be prepared to defend it. It is through twofiveone that I have begun to shape my own identity, and find what I want from technology. For me, it is a means of sharing your story to the world, to communicate to billions of people instantaneously, and best of all, share information for free.

The internet began as an open platform. Twofiveone is one such website maintaining that standard: being a website, it cannot be sold, change ownership, be silenced, or monetised. Great things happen when we share information for free, it can change lives. It certainly changed mine.
